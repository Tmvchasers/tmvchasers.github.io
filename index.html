About Me

I’m TMV Chaser Chris, a storm chaser, content creator, and author based in Nebraska. My passion is documenting real storms, real moments, and real-life stories as they happen.

Storm chasing is more than just watching weather. It takes patience, radar tracking, planning, and respect for how dangerous severe weather can be. I built this site as a home base for my journey — a place where people can follow my content, check live radar, and support my books.

Along with storm chasing, I create gaming videos, music, streams, and creative projects. My goal is to keep growing, keep learning, and keep building something real from the ground up.

I’m also the author of My Life But Different, a book about life, pressure, growth, and becoming stronger through everything life throws at you.

Mission: document real storms, create real content, and turn pressure into progress.
